# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone.

## Expected Behavior
- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus feedback on ideas, code, and protocol quality.
- Assume good intent and collaborate in good faith.
- Help keep discussions useful, clear, and inclusive.

## Unacceptable Behavior
- Harassment, threats, or discrimination.
- Personal attacks, insults, or sustained hostility.
- Doxxing or sharing private information without consent.
- Disruptive trolling, spam, or bad-faith participation.

## Enforcement
Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this policy. They may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation for behavior that violates this Code of Conduct.

## Reporting
Report conduct issues privately to repository maintainers through GitHub private channels when possible. Include links, timestamps, and context so maintainers can investigate promptly.

## Consequences
Depending on severity, outcomes may include a warning, temporary suspension, or permanent removal from project spaces.
